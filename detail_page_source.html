Overview
GREATER SOUTHERN WATERFRONT LIVING

Five times the size of Marina Bay, Singapore’s next great waterfront living will connect you to Marina Bay, Jurong Lake and no less than 13 parks. Two distinctive 5-storey blocks woven into the topography of the land and bracketed by pools and paths, The Hillshore is where the city meets the greater outdoors. Located just minutes away from the nearest MRT station, homeowners will enjoy easy access to Mapletree Business City, Vivocity and the Central Business District, making it a hub for convenient and cosmopolitan living.
